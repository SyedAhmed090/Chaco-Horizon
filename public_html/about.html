Who We Are

Chaco Horizon General Trading L.L.C. is a diversified general trading company licensed by the Department of Economic Development, Dubai, under Trade Licence 1653213, operating from the Gold Building in Al Ras, Deira — the historic heart of Dubai's wholesale trade.

Our portfolio spans spices, dry fruits, coffee and green & black tea; solar panels, inverters, batteries and LED lights; cars, tyres and auto parts; and garments and glassware — supplied to wholesalers, distributors, installers, retailers and workshops across the UAE and beyond. As our clients' needs grow, so does our range.

Gold Building, Al Ras, Deira · Dubai, UAE

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to be the trading partner businesses rely on — delivering quality products at competitive prices, with professional service, complete documentation and on-time delivery on every order.

Our vision is to grow Chaco Horizon into one of Dubai's most trusted diversified trading houses, connecting producers and manufacturers with buyers across the Gulf, Africa and Asia through long-term relationships built on trust.
