A Diversified General Trading Company in the Heart of Dubai

Chaco Horizon General Trading L.L.C. is a Dubai-licensed general trading company (Trade Licence 1653213) headquartered in Deira, the centre of the city's wholesale trade. We import, export and distribute a growing portfolio of products across four categories: Food & Agri, Energy & Solar, Automotive, and Consumer Goods.

Whether you need a container of spices or tea, a pallet of solar panels, a shipment of cars and tyres or a consignment of garments, you deal with one licensed partner, one set of paperwork and one dedicated point of contact.
