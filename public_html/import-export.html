Shipping terms explained

Flexible Incoterms to Suit Your Business

We ship on the terms that work best for you. The difference is simply where our responsibility ends and yours begins. EXW and CFR are also available on request.

FOB

Free on Board

We deliver the goods loaded onto the vessel at the port of origin. You arrange sea freight, insurance and onward delivery — ideal if you have your own freight rates.

You control freight
CIF

Cost, Insurance & Freight

We cover the goods, sea freight and marine insurance to your named port. You handle import duty, clearance and inland delivery.

To your port
DAP

Delivered at Place

We deliver to your named site or warehouse; you handle import duty and clearance. Popular for road deliveries within the Gulf.

To your door
DDP

Delivered Duty Paid

We deliver to your door with duties and clearance settled — the simplest option for you, available for selected destinations.

Fully managed

Part containers ship as LCL; urgent or high-value items can travel by air. Whatever the mode, the documentation and quality checks are the same.
